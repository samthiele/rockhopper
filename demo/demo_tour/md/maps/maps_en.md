# Maps

A georeferenced image can be draped on a terrain mesh. This stop is `island.tif`. Heights come from elevation tiles, and the mesh keeps small triangles only where the slope changes. Elevation is drawn at 2.5× so the relief is easier to see.

The stop opens on the orthoimagery. The buttons at the top left switch colour on the same mesh:

- **esri** is orthoimagery for this ground
- **island** is the source GeoTIFF
- **elevation** colours the surface by height, from low (purple) to high (yellow)
